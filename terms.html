2. Description of the service

Sarah is an assistant that replies to the people who have contacted the Client's agency, on the Client's messaging channels, qualifies them, books a viewing or hands the enquiry over according to the Client's process.

The service is inbound only: Sarah replies to the people who wrote to the agency. She never contacts people who have not reached out, sends no cold messages and does no outbound prospecting.

Sarah does not replace the Client's team or software. She sits on top of the tools the Client already uses. The Client stays in control at all times: they can switch her on, switch her off, adjust the service or take over a conversation.

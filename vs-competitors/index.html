Who they really are

For an informed decision, here's the honest breakdown of every solution.

🇨🇦 Montréal · US market

Roof AI

Hidden pricing — book a demo

Founded 2016. AI chatbot for the websites of large US brokerages (RE/MAX, Briggs Freeman, Berkshire Hathaway). Specialty: MLS-native search, Fair Housing compliance, 100+ customers claimed.

Bootstrapped (only $350K raised) — team ~10–15 people. Estimated revenue ~$2M ARR.

For you: if you're not a US brokerage with 50+ agents, they're not for you. No EU presence, no WhatsApp, no voice.
🇺🇸 US · acquired by Verse.io

Structurely

$179–499/month (50–225 leads/month)

Aisa Holmes chatbot: SMS + web chat + Facebook Messenger qualification. 5,000+ teams as customers. Recently acquired by Verse.io.

Tier-based pricing capped on lead volume (overage at scale) or pay-per-lead at $3/lead.

For you: SMS-first, not aligned with European WhatsApp habits. Pay-per-lead = uncontrolled overspend on busy weeks.
🇫🇷 France · independent

MonAgent-IA

€99 to €199/month

WhatsApp chatbot for FR estate agencies. Apimo, Hektor, Périclès integrations. Sales-driven tone.

Reported stats: 3× leads, 40% conversion lift, 50% qualification time reduction. Testimonial from Immo Premium Lyon.

For you: low entry ticket, but the scope stops at the WhatsApp chatbot. No voice, no negotiator co-pilot, no operational follow-up post setup.
🇫🇷 France · established CRM

Hektor (Deep Flow Realty)

On request · enterprise

Leading French real estate CRM with the Deep Flow Realty AI module: opportunity scoring, optimal call timing suggestions.

Not an AI overlay — a CRM with an AI layer. Partial substitute.

For you: if you already use Hektor, Sarah Studio plugs on top. Hektor manages the pipeline, Sarah qualifies live on WhatsApp + voice + co-pilot dashboard.

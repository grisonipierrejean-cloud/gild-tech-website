The 5 questions every director asks us.

If yours isn't here, ask it directly during the free audit.

Will my buyers feel they're talking to a robot?

Sarah asks 1 relevant question then sends a Calendly link. It's faster than a human and more practical for the buyer — they pick their own slot instead of waiting for your callback. Wording is adjustable, the tone is human, the WhatsApp number is yours. You keep full control of the conversation.

What if Sarah misunderstands a reply?

Sarah automatically escalates to your team on your usual channel (Slack, Teams or email) if she's in doubt. You see the full conversation and can take over in 1 click. Escalations are rare (emotional situations, off-script questions) and each one trains Sarah further.

PDPA and compliance?

Sarah sends via your WhatsApp Business number with your branding. PDPA-compliant (Thailand Personal Data Protection Act, in force since June 2022, mirrors GDPR). Data processing agreement with GILD-TECH signed at kick-off. You remain Data Controller. Data stored in your Google Workspace, not ours.

Does it work with my CRM (HubSpot / Pipedrive / custom)?

Sarah doesn't replace your CRM, she sits upstream. She catches enquiries as they arrive, qualifies, books the viewing. Once the buyer signs an offer, you push them into your CRM as before. Sarah handles the pre-offer phase that real estate CRMs typically cover lightly.

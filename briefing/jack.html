💰 ROI calculation live (10 min)

Pull up gild-tech.com/calculateur?lang=en on your screen. Plug in their numbers in real time. Walk through the calculation OUT LOUD so they see the logic, not just the number:

The 5-step calculation — explain each step

Step 1 — Volume of enquiries lost
[X] monthly enquiries × 67% (NAR 2023: never followed up)
= [N] enquiries dropped each month

Step 2 — Instructions lost on those enquiries
[N] × [conv]% (their conversion rate, default 5%)
= [M] instructions never won (the realistic ones, not the dreams)

Step 3 — Opportunity cost in £
[M] × £[Z] (their average sole agency fee)
= £[A] monthly OPPORTUNITY LOSS — this is what's slipping through

Step 4 — Realistic recovery with Sarah
£[A] × ~35% (Sarah recovers a portion, not all — applicants who've gone cold can't all be saved)
= £[B] actually recovered each month

Step 5 — ROI
Pro at £2,290 / £[B per day] = [P] days to break even
Scale at £4,590 / £[B per day] = [Q] days to break even
Be honest about the 35% recovery rate. The full £[A] is the opportunity cost — what's leaking. Sarah doesn't catch every drop — she catches the ones who haven't gone fully cold. That's typically 30-40%. Quoting 100% recovery makes you look greedy and breaks credibility. Quoting 35% makes you look honest and the numbers still land hard.

🤐 Closing rules — silence + the 3-NO rule

Silence is your BEST friend. Not your "friend" — your best friend. After every price, every option, every question — hold the silence. Most closers lose deals because they fill the silence with extra justification the prospect didn't ask for.

The "3 NO" rule — when to actually leave

A prospect saying "no" once doesn't mean they mean it. They might mean "convince me", "I don't get it", "I need to feel safe". You handle each "no" with a calm question, not a sell.

  • 1st NO — "OK. Just to understand: what specifically isn't working for you — the price, the timing, the technical complexity, or the actual usefulness?" Listen. Address it.
  • 2nd NO — "Fair. Last question: is it that you don't want to solve this problem, or is it that this isn't the right way to solve it?" Listen. Reframe.
  • 3rd NO — "Got it. Thanks for being upfront. I'll let you go. If anything changes in the next 6 months, you have my number." Hang up. Don't push.
Pushing past the 3rd NO ruins your reputation in the UK estate agency market — it's a small world, principals talk to each other. The prospect who says "no" today might call you back in 4 months saying "remember that thing you proposed?". The prospect you bullied past 3 NOs will badmouth you to two competitors.

"Hi [First name], thanks for being on the call. The reason I'm here today: we've found that 67% of property enquiries from Rightmove, Zoopla or your website are never followed up — that's NAR 2023 data. Not because UK estate agencies are bad, but because the form arrives at 10pm Sunday and the applicant has called 3 competitors before you see it Monday morning.

What I'm going to do in the next 30 minutes: I'll ask you 10 questions about how your sales process works today, calculate with you exactly how much that's costing you each month, and show you live what we install at independent UK estate agencies to fix it. At the end, you tell me if it makes sense for your agency. If not, I leave you alone."

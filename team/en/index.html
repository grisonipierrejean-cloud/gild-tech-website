🧱 Objection comebacks

"It's too expensive"
Sarah pays for herself from the first instruction recovered (average commission £8,000). You lose 6 a month. Net: +£10,310. 3 monthly instalments available.
"I already use a CRM (Reapit / Alto / Jupix)"
We plug on top, we don't replace. Sarah reads your CRM, writes qualified leads back into it. You change nothing.
"Prospects will feel it's a bot"
Sarah asks 1 question, sends a Calendly link. 60% click rate vs 5% on a classic callback. Escalation to a human if she doubts.
"What about Roof AI?"
Roof AI is built for US brokerages with MLS and Fair Housing. No EU presence, no WhatsApp, no voice. Different planet.
"I want to think about it"
No worries. 2 quick questions: (1) what specifically is making you hesitate? (2) shall we book a follow-up in 3 days, or shall I drop you? (forces a binary)
"GDPR?"
Fully compliant. Your number, your branding, your Google Workspace. You remain DPO. Data Processing Agreement signed.

1 · Pattern interrupts · first 30 seconds

Goal: break the prospect's expectation in the first second. No polite classic intro. 3 expert-level techniques used by top B2B closers (Sandler · Cole Gordon · Jeremy Miner / NEPQ).

#1 · UP FRONT CONTRACT · Sandler School (1967)

Hi [First name], this is [Your name] from GILD-TECH. Here's how this is going to work: 90 seconds. If at the end it doesn't make sense for you, just say "not for me", we hang up no hard feelings. If it does, we keep going for 2 more minutes. Deal?
Why it's expert: you set the rules. The prospect no longer wonders how much time he's going to lose — he accepts or declines. Firm + respectful. Sandler signature technique since 1967.
When to use: default, 50% of calls. Particularly effective on UK / European agency owners on Koh Samui who appreciate structure.

#2 · STATUS FRAME · Cole Gordon (NEPQ)

[Your name] from GILD-TECH. I'm not sure we should work together — depends on your situation. 3 short questions to check. If I'm wasting your time, I'll stop. OK?
Why it's expert: you're NOT a salesperson begging. You're an expert qualifying. The prospect has to prove HE is eligible — not the other way around. Cole Gordon (coach to $500k/year closers) popularised it. Completely inverts the power dynamic.
When to use: "jaded" prospects who get 5 cold callers a week. Also for top players (large established agencies) who think they have nothing to learn.

#3 · PERMISSION WITH STAKES · forced binary

Hi [First name]. 90 seconds. Two scenarios: either I waste your time, or I save you from leaving $60,000 USD on the table this year. Want to find out which one?
Why it's expert: you present both possible outcomes. Curiosity to know "which of the two" forces the prospect to continue. The concrete number ($60k = MIT Sloan 21× × avg Koh Samui villa commission) anchors value from second one.
When to use: when you have the energy to deliver with confidence. Warning: if prospect says "go on, show me", you MUST deliver concrete numbers in the next 60 seconds (avg Koh Samui villa price × commission × MIT Sloan multiplier).
Golden rule: if any response — whatever it is — transition to the 90-second pitch (section 3). If hard hang-up: "Not available" in CRM, recall in 6 months. Never insist.

4 · Top 3 objections · verbatim + psychology

We already list on FazWaz / Lazudi / Hipflat
Of course — every serious Koh Samui agency does. Question though: when a buyer clicks your listing on Lazudi at 9 PM Sunday from London, does FazWaz reply for you in under 5 minutes via WhatsApp, in English, without a human touching it? Portals do lead aggregation, not sub-5-minute reply. That's what we install on top. 30 minutes, I'll show you.
Why it works: you never attack the portals (they're invested in them). You pivot to one specific capability NO portal does. 3-condition closed question (5 min · WhatsApp · without human) = he can't honestly say yes.
How much?
Two tiers: Pro at $2,490 USD for agencies with 1-5 agents, Scale at $4,990 for structures with 5+ and active prospecting. But the right choice depends on your specific situation — that's exactly what we work out in the audit. Audit's free, you'll tell me at the end.
Why it works: you give the number directly (refusing burns trust). But you immediately pivot to "depends on your situation" → routes to the audit where you defend value in context. Never defend a price in a vacuum — always against quantified missed revenue.
We're not interested
Fair enough. Just to understand: is that because you've already solved the sub-5-minute reply problem, or it's not a priority right now? […] Got it — appreciate your time, have a great day.
Why it works: it's a NEPQ qualification loop (Jeremy Miner). 90% of "not interested" responses are automatic, not thoughtful. Your question forces real reflection. If they say "already solved", ask "how?" — usually it's not true and they realise as they explain. If they say "not a priority", exit clean + recall in 6 months.
Absolute rule: after a clear and conscious NO, never push. CRM = "Not interested", recall in 6 months. Pressure burns the relationship for 24 months.

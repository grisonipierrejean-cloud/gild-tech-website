About us

An operator team. Not a startup talking about digital transformation.

GILD-TECH is the tool we wished we'd had when we were closing on the phone ourselves. 19 years of calling pipelines made us realise no off-the-shelf tool actually fits the day-to-day of an independent estate agent. So we built one.

Our angle.

The French and UK real estate market is served by two types of solutions: heavy CRMs (Apimo, Hektor, Reapit, Alto) calibrated to structure a pipeline but blind to real-time response, and generic chatbots that have never qualified an applicant in their life.

Our take: an operational system that plugs on top of your existing stack, qualifies in real time on WhatsApp or by phone, and partners with your team for 30 days post go-live. Not yet another tool. An execution layer that turns dormant enquiries into signed instructions.

Our 3 principles.

1. We say what we do. We do what we say.

No vague promise of "digital transformation". You know exactly what Sarah does (qualifies, follows up, briefs) and what she doesn't (negotiates an instruction, signs a deal). If Sarah doesn't deliver within 30 days, you get 100% of your payment back.

2. We plug in, we don't replace.

Your CRM (Apimo, Hektor, Reapit, Alto…) remains your source of truth. Your negotiators keep using their usual tools. Sarah adds a smart execution layer on top — not another piece of software to manage.

3. We're with you daily for the first 30 days.

The value isn't in the setup, it's in the operational tuning post go-live. Direct hotline, weekly debriefs, ongoing script calibration based on your feedback. We stay partners until Sarah runs without intervention.

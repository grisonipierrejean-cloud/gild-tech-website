Will my buyers feel they’re talking to a robot?

The tone is human, the wording is adjustable, the WhatsApp number is yours. You keep full control of the conversation and take it back in one click whenever you want.

What if Sarah misreads a reply?

Sarah alerts you on WhatsApp, on your own number, or by email, the moment she is in doubt. You see the full conversation and take over in 1 click. Escalations are rare (emotional situations, unusual properties) and each one trains Sarah for next time.

GDPR and IPI professional conduct?

Sarah sends from the WhatsApp number dedicated to your agency, with your branding. The data processing agreement with GILD-TECH is signed at kick-off. You remain Data Controller, we remain processor. No fabricated property, no fabricated price: your negotiator gives the valuation, on site, under your IPI licence.

Does it work with my CRM (Whise / SweepBright / Omnicasa / Hovi)?

Sarah does not replace your CRM: she plugs into it if it has an API. Otherwise, a tracking sheet is provided: every lead and every follow-up is logged there. Sarah handles the upstream phase, the one real estate CRMs cover lightly. For reporting we use your CRM connectors, otherwise we stay on a clean Google Sheet.

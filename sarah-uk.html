The 5 questions every director asks us.

If yours isn't here, ask it directly during the free audit.

Will my prospects feel they're talking to a robot?

Sarah asks 1 relevant question then sends a Calendly link. It's faster than a human and more practical for the prospect. Wording is adjustable, the tone is human. 60% of qualified prospects click the Calendly link — vs 5% on a classic phone callback.

What if Sarah misunderstands a reply?

Sarah automatically escalates to your team on Slack if she's in doubt. You see the full conversation and can take over in 1 click. Out of 100 enquiries, on average 5 are escalated (emotional situations, off-script questions).

GDPR and compliance?

Sarah sends via your WhatsApp Business number with your branding. The data processing agreement with GILD-TECH is signed at kick-off. You remain Data Controller. Data stored in your Google Workspace, not ours.

Does it work with my CRM (Reapit / Alto / Jupix)?

Sarah doesn't replace your CRM — she sits upstream. She catches enquiries as they arrive, qualifies, books the viewing. Once the prospect signs an instruction agreement, you push them into your CRM as before. Sarah handles the pre-instruction phase where estate agency CRMs are weak.

How long before Sarah is live for me?

1 week maximum. Technical deployment on GILD-TECH's side takes 4 hours. The remaining days are for: configuring your Calendly, testing with your team, validating the wording. The first real lead is handled by Sarah by the Friday after signature at the latest.

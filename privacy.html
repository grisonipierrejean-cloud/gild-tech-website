Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

GILD-TECH builds Sarah, an assistant that answers buyers on behalf of real estate agencies on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who we share it with, and how you can exercise your rights or have your data deleted.

1. Who we are

Girlden LTD, trading as GILD-TECH

Company registered in England and Wales, number 14837617

Registered office: 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom

Email: ops@gild-tech.com

2. Two different roles

  • For our clients and prospects (real estate agencies and their staff), Girlden LTD is the data controller.
  • For the buyers and sellers who contact an agency that uses Sarah, the agency is the data controller and Girlden LTD is its data processor. We act only on the agency's documented instructions, under a data processing agreement signed at the start of the service.

3. Data we process

About agencies (as controller): name, job title, company, email, phone, the content of our exchanges, billing data, and browsing data on our website (see our cookie policy).

About buyers and sellers who message an agency (as processor). When someone writes to an agency on WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram, comments on the agency's Facebook or Instagram post or ad, or fills in the agency's website form, Sarah processes for that agency:

  • the phone number or Meta account identifier, and the profile name;
  • the content of messages and comments, including voice notes (transcribed to text) and media sent;
  • what the person tells Sarah about their search: property of interest, budget, timeline, preferred language;
  • appointment details (date, time, agent);
  • the history of the conversation and of the follow-ups.

4. Why we process it

  • to reply to the person on behalf of the agency, in their language;
  • to qualify the request, book a viewing or pass the file to the agency's negotiator;
  • to send follow-up messages within the rules of the messaging platform: outside the 24-hour customer service window, only message templates approved by Meta;
  • to give the agency a record of each conversation (a tracking sheet, or the agency's CRM when it has an API);
  • for agencies: to provide and bill our service, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use buyers' and sellers' data for our own marketing or for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

  • Buyers' and sellers' data: for as long as the agency's contract with us lasts, then deleted or returned to the agency within 30 days of the end of the contract, unless the agency asks for earlier deletion.
  • Agencies' prospect data: 3 years from the last contact.
  • Client data: the length of the contract, then the period required by law.
  • Cookies: 13 months maximum.

7. How to delete your data

To delete the data linked to a conversation with an agency that uses Sarah (on WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, or through a comment), send an email to ops@gild-tech.com with the subject Delete my data, and include:

  • your phone number (WhatsApp), your Facebook name (Messenger) or your Instagram username;
  • the name of the agency you contacted.

We delete the data and confirm it to you within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to ops@gild-tech.com. If you contacted an agency, you can also ask the agency directly; we help the agency answer. We reply within one month.

9. Complaint

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom (ico.org.uk), or to your local data protection authority in the European Union, for example the Data Protection Authority in Belgium (dataprotectionauthority.be) or the CNIL in France (cnil.fr).

10. Security

Access to data is limited to the people who need it. Access keys are stored outside our code, and connections are encrypted.

11. Changes

We update this page when our practices change. The date of the last update is shown at the top.

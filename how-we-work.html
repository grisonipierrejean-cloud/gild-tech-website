Our method

How we work.

You are not buying software, you are adding someone to your team. Here is exactly how it goes.

Before

A conversation, not a demo.

A 30-minute conversation. We look at the buyers you lost recently, and where they went.

If you do not have a response-time problem, I will tell you and we stop there.

The install

I do it myself.

You have nothing to install, nothing to learn, no tool to change.

What I need from you: how you reply, your qualifying questions, your hours, and what must reach you straight away.

Sarah learns your listings, your usual replies, your tone. She sends nothing you have not read and approved.

Day-to-day

She replies, you command.

She answers your buyers, day and night, in their language. She sorts, she logs, she books the viewing or passes the file per your procedure.

You are in command. You switch her on, you switch her off, you change her hours or her questions whenever you want. If you would rather take over a conversation, you take it back.

In the evening, you sit down, you open the dashboard, it is all there.

When it goes off script

It will happen.

An off-topic question, a buyer who writes something unexpected.

Sarah hands over to a human the moment she is in doubt. She would rather stay quiet than say something wrong.

And if the problem is on our side, you do not file a ticket. You write to me, and I answer.

It is your name on the shopfront, not mine.

What we do not sell

The limits, said upfront.

  • Sarah prospects no one. She answers the people who wrote to you. Never a cold message.
  • She never speaks to your owners. The sellers are yours.
  • She replaces neither your team nor your software. She sits on top of what you already use.
  • We do not sell what is not built. If a feature is in progress, we tell you, we do not bill it.
If it is not for you

You leave, no friction.

30-day money-back. Cancel anytime. Your data stays yours and you leave with it.

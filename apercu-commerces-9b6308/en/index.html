It's the person who keeps your business alive online: they reply to your customers, post for you and keep an eye on your reputation.

With Renomia, it's a service: it works every week, and nothing goes out outside the rules you've set.

Your questions.

Won't an AI just write anything?

No. About your business, it only writes what's in your Renomia brief, the one you fill in when you start (see the extract above). A negative review is first sent to you; if you haven't replied within 4 hours, a person at Renomia publishes a factual, calm reply. A serious review is always handled by a person.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

Never. You add us as a manager and remove us whenever you like.

How long before I see results?

Replies to your reviews and your posts start in the first week. On the number of reviews, we commit: 10 new Google reviews in 60 days, or we keep working for free until you have them (cards on display, business open). Your rating and your position on Google depend on what your customers write and on Google: nobody can honestly guarantee those.

Do you only ask happy customers for reviews?

No. We ask every customer for a review, with no filtering: Google's rules forbid soliciting only positive reviews. A customer who prefers to write to you privately can do so, but the link to Google is always shown, to everyone. And nobody sets a review quota for your staff: Google forbids that too.

What if I don't like a reply?

For a 1- to 3-star review, you edit the reply or reject it before it's published. If you haven't replied within 4 hours, we publish a factual, calm reply, so your customer gets an answer within 24 hours. For a 4- or 5-star review, the reply goes out without disturbing you, following the written rules in your brief: what we can say about you, and what we never say.

How are the videos made?

You film a few clips on your phone, 5 to 20 seconds each: the filming guide in your account tells you what to shoot. We edit them for Reels and TikTok: cuts, text, subtitles, your style. No filming? Your photos become a punchy slideshow. Music is added from the networks' approved libraries when possible. Not happy with a video? Tell us what to change and we re-edit it. You only film people who agree to appear, and nothing is published without your approval.

What if I don't approve in time?

A post or video you haven't approved or rejected 48 hours after we send it is published as it is: your account never goes quiet. You can always reject it before then, in one click.

Why a set-up fee?

It pays for the work at the start: in-depth audit, listing optimisation, on-site filming, QR and NFC review cards. €190 excl. VAT, once. Waived for any subscription taken out up to and including 30 November 2026.

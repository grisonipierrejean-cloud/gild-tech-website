Your questions.

Won't an AI just write anything?

No. It only writes what's in your brief. Negative reviews always go through you.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

Never. You add us as a manager and remove us whenever you like.

How long before I see results?

Replies to your reviews and your posts start in the first week. Your rating and your position on Google move with new reviews, and that takes several weeks. No result is guaranteed: your customers write what they want, and it's Google that ranks the listings.

What if I don't like a reply?

For a 1- to 3-star review, nothing goes out without you: you edit the reply or reject it before it's published. For a 4- or 5-star review, the reply goes out without disturbing you, following the written rules in your brief: what we can say about you, and what we never say.

Why not my nephew or a freelancer?

What changes with RENOM: consistency, every week, without depending on one person's availability. A report every month, on the 1st, by email. And written rules for your replies, which you set in your brief.

What about my Trustpilot reviews?

Yes, as an option on any plan: we prepare each reply, you publish it with a copy and paste from your Trustpilot account. Just tell us during the audit.

Do you already have clients?

We're taking on our first clients. No made-up testimonials: the audit is free and your first month is refunded if you're not satisfied.

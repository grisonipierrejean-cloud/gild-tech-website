5. VAT

The Supplier is established in the United Kingdom, outside the European Union. The Service is a supply of services to a taxable person established in France: it is deemed to take place in France (article 259, 1° of the French General Tax Code; article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC) and French VAT is payable by the Client, as the recipient, under the reverse-charge mechanism (article 283, 2 of the French General Tax Code, deriving from article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC). Invoices are therefore issued excluding VAT and bear the mention “Autoliquidation” (reverse charge).

The Client provides its SIREN number and, if it has one, its VAT identification number. It declares the VAT due on these invoices under its own scheme; the Supplier does not collect it. A Client that falls under the VAT franchise scheme (franchise en base) checks the treatment that applies to it with its adviser.

Supplier's VAT number: Not registered for VAT (supplier not established in France; reverse charge applied by the Client).

9. Reviews: rules applied

  • No fake reviews. The Supplier never writes reviews, either for the Client or against a competitor, and refuses any request to do so.
  • No selection. Every customer is asked for a review, without selecting them according to their satisfaction; the review page is identical whatever the rating and the link to Google is always shown, including when the customer chooses to write privately. No benefit is given in exchange for a review. Neither the Supplier nor the Client sets the business's staff a number of reviews to obtain, and no customer is pressed to leave a review on the premises. These rules follow Google's rules for contributions to Google Maps.
  • A serious review (accusation, threat, sensitive subject) is never handled automatically: it is flagged to the Client and handled by a person.
  • Replies to reviews. By default, replies to 4- and 5-star reviews are published without prior approval. Replies to 1- to 3-star reviews are proposed to the Client as soon as the review arrives, and the Client is notified; it approves, edits or rejects them. If the Client has not replied 4 hours after the review arrived, the Supplier itself publishes a factual, calm reply, within the rules of the Client's brief. The Client may change this setting at any time in its client area. Posts on social networks and on the Google listing follow the tacit approval rule of section 8.
  • Every review request email states that it is sent on the Client's behalf and includes an unsubscribe link, which is honoured for all subsequent requests.

10. Guarantees

Reply within 24 hours. Every Google review posted on the Client's listing receives a published reply within 24 hours of its arrival in the Service, which checks for new reviews every 15 minutes, under the rules of section 9. A serious review is never handled automatically: a person replies to it, within the same time limit. This guarantee assumes the Client has given the Supplier manager access to its listing; it is suspended during an outage or a decision by Google that prevents replying.

10 new Google reviews in 60 days. The Supplier guarantees the Client 10 new Google reviews on its listing within 60 days of set-up. Otherwise, it continues to provide the Service free of charge, without invoicing the subscription, until the 10 new reviews are obtained. This guarantee gives rise to no refund; for payment in advance, the paid period is extended by the number of free months.

Conditions: the review cards provided by the Supplier are on display, visible to customers, from set-up and throughout the period; the business stays open over the period, a closure of more than 7 consecutive days extending the period by the same length; the subscription is paid. Measurement: the number of reviews shown on the Client's Google listing on set-up day, read from a dated screenshot given to the Client, compared with the number on day 60, read in the same way; only reviews visible on the listing on the day of measurement count. The guarantee applies once per business, at the first set-up. It binds the Supplier alone: no number of reviews is asked of the Client's staff (section 9).

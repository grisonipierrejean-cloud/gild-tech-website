5. VAT

The Supplier is established in the United Kingdom, outside the European Union. The Service is a supply of services to a taxable person established in France: it is deemed to take place in France (article 259, 1° of the French General Tax Code; article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC) and French VAT is payable by the Client, as the recipient, under the reverse-charge mechanism (article 283, 2 of the French General Tax Code, deriving from article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC). Invoices are therefore issued excluding VAT and bear the mention “Autoliquidation” (reverse charge).

The Client provides its SIREN number and, if it has one, its VAT identification number. It declares the VAT due on these invoices under its own scheme; the Supplier does not collect it. A Client that falls under the VAT franchise scheme (franchise en base) checks the treatment that applies to it with its adviser.

Supplier's VAT number: Not registered for VAT (supplier not established in France; reverse charge applied by the Client).

7. Term, no minimum commitment, termination

The subscription is monthly, with no minimum term. It renews each month until it is cancelled.

The Client may cancel at any time, at no cost and without giving a reason, by email to ops@gild-tech.com or by replying to an email from the Service. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current month, which has already been invoiced; no further month is due.

The Supplier may terminate with 30 days' notice. It may terminate without notice if the Client asks it to produce fake reviews, to select which customers are asked for a review, or to publish unlawful content, or if the Client fails to pay after the suspension provided for in section 6.

At the end of the contract, the Supplier stops any scheduled post that has not yet gone out, sends no further email on the Client's behalf and stops using its access. The Client removes the access it has granted (manager of the Google listing, connected social networks). What happens to the data follows the data processing agreement.

9. Reviews: rules applied

  • No fake reviews. The Supplier never writes reviews, either for the Client or against a competitor, and refuses any request to do so.
  • No selection. The review request is the same for all imported customers, without selecting them according to their satisfaction, and the review page is identical whatever the rating. No benefit is given in exchange for a review.
  • A serious review (accusation, threat, sensitive subject) is never handled automatically: it is flagged to the Client and handled by a person.
  • Nothing is published without the Client's agreement. Every post on social networks and on the Google listing is approved by the Client. For replies to reviews, the Client chooses in its client area: by default, replies to 4- and 5-star reviews are published without prior approval and all others are submitted to the Client; the Client may change this setting at any time.
  • Every review request email states that it is sent on the Client's behalf and includes an unsubscribe link, which is honoured for all subsequent requests.

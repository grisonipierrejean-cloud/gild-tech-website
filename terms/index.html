3.Description of Services

Girlden LTD offers three main automation plans for independent estate agencies:

3.1 Starter plan

  • Sarah, 24/7 WhatsApp virtual receptionist
  • Connection to 1 portal (Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket or Boomin — Client's choice)
  • Real estate-optimised generic scripts
  • Real-time dashboard
  • 30 days of included usage

3.2 Pro plan

  • Everything in Starter
  • Personalised scripts for the agency (audit + director interview)
  • 3 portals connected + integration with 1 CRM (Reapit, Alto, Jupix, Apimo or Hektor — Client's choice)
  • Sarah Daily Brief (WhatsApp 7am + detailed email)
  • Auto callback under 5 min · Follow-ups D+3 / D+7 / D+14
  • Automated weekly review Monday 8am
  • Daily operational partnership for 30 days

3.3 Scale plan

  • Everything in Pro
  • Sarah Voice 24/7 (AI phone agent)
  • Multi-negotiator morning brief
  • Automated FSBO sweep (Rightmove + Zoopla)
  • Expired listings detection
  • 12-month dormant database wake-up
  • Viewing prep sheet sent 1 hour before each appointment
  • Multi-CRM or custom ERP

3.4 Optional maintenance

After the initial 30-day partnership, the Client may subscribe to a monthly maintenance plan including ongoing monitoring, on-demand tuning, and new features automatically deployed.

5.Delivery and timeline

The standard go-live timeline for Pro and Scale plans spans 7 working days between signature and switch to production, followed by 30 days of daily operational partnership.

  • Day 0: strategic audit (30-minute video call)
  • Days 1-5: deep diagnostic and bespoke architecture (Girlden LTD side)
  • Days 5-7: configuration and internal testing (Girlden LTD side)
  • Day 7: lightning go-live (4 hours on-site or remote) + team onboarding (30 minutes)
  • Days 8-37: daily operational partnership

This timeline may be adjusted based on Client availability and the complexity of their technical ecosystem. Any significant delay is subject to written notification.
